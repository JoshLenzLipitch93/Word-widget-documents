Frequently Asked Questions

How does the app blocker work?

Linguease uses Screen Time to sit between you and the apps you choose. When you open a selected app, you'll be asked to practise a vocabulary word before continuing. You can customise which apps trigger this and how many words to practise.

How do I set up the home screen widget?

Long-press on your home screen, tap the "+" button in the top corner, search for "Linguease," and choose your preferred widget size. The widget displays your active vocabulary words throughout the day.

Which languages are supported?

Linguease currently supports learning vocabulary in Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, and Irish from English.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Translations are powered by on-device machine learning. After the initial language download, no internet connection is needed.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions. Find Linguease and tap Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How does spaced repetition work?

Words you complete come back for review at increasing intervals. This is based on proven memory science — you review words just before you would forget them, which moves them into long-term memory.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Send us an email at joshlenzlipitch@gmail.com with a description of the issue and your device model. Screenshots are always helpful.
