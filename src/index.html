Good to know

A few straight answers.

How is this different from Duolingo?

Duolingo is a study session you sit down for. Linguease is the opposite — it uses the time you were going to waste anyway. No streaks, no pressure, no gamification. If you already do a lesson a day, this sits alongside it rather than replacing it.

Does the gate use Screen Time?

No. Linguease uses a Siri Shortcuts personal automation to put the word in front of the app you're opening. You set it up once in the Shortcuts app and Linguease walks you through it. It never asks for the Family Controls permission.

Which apps can I put it in front of?

Any app you can name in Shortcuts. Most people pick Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Reddit and Facebook, but nothing is hard-coded.

Is it only Brazilian Portuguese?

That's what it's built around and where the word lists are strongest. Fifty other languages are available in the picker, European Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Dutch and Irish among them.

Is it free?

The widget and basic translations are free. Phonetics, every language, and unlimited words are part of the subscription. Cancel any time from iPhone Settings.

iPhone only?

Yes, for now — iOS 16 and above. The whole product is the home-screen widget and the Shortcuts gate, and both are iPhone-specific.
