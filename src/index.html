A separate feature

There is also a home-screen widget.

Nothing to do with the gate, and it works on its own. Add the Linguease widget to your home screen in small, medium or large, and it holds one word in front of you between openings. It changes colour on a spaced-repetition cycle so it never quietly becomes wallpaper. Twenty-four themes, and words you have nailed rotate out.

Good to know

A few straight answers.

Does the gate use Screen Time?

No. Linguease uses a Siri Shortcuts personal automation to put the word in front of the app you are opening. You set it up once in the Shortcuts app and Linguease walks you through it. It never asks for the Family Controls permission.

Which apps can I put it in front of?

Most of them. Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, Reddit and Facebook all work, and so do plenty of others. It depends on whether Shortcuts can see the app, so a few will not work. Nothing is hard-coded, so it is worth trying the one you have in mind.

What if I am in a hurry?

Skip it. There is a dismiss on every gate and no penalty for using it. The point is the words you do type, not the ones you do not.

How is this different from Duolingo?

Duolingo is a study session you sit down for. Linguease is the opposite: it uses the time you were going to waste anyway. No streaks, no pressure, no gamification. If you already do a lesson a day, this sits alongside it rather than replacing it.

Is it only Brazilian Portuguese?

That is what it is built around and where the word lists are strongest. Fifty other languages are available in the picker, European Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Dutch and Irish among them.

Is it free?

The widget and basic translations are free. Phonetics, every language, and unlimited words are part of the subscription. Cancel any time from iPhone Settings.

iPhone only?

Yes, for now, iOS 16 and above. The whole product is the Shortcuts gate and the home-screen widget, and both are iPhone-specific.
